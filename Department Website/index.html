About Department

The Department of Economics provides undergraduate education following the curriculum of Delhi School of Economics to develop analytical and economic thinking.

Faculty

Department faculty members:

  • Mr. S. Krishnakumar
  • Mr. D. Brahma Reddy
  • Dr. Sheebani Goswami

Courses Offered

  • B.A. (Hons) Economics: Undergraduate program focusing on economic theory, mathematics, and statistics.
  • B.A. Programme (Economics): Undergraduate discipline course covering fundamentals of microeconomics and economic development.
  • Generic Electives (GE): Introductory economics courses offered to students of other departments.

Activities

  • Blisspoint: The annual academic and cultural festival organized by the Economics Association.
  • Econ Expressions: The annual student-run departmental journal publishing student articles.
  • Seminars: Academic lectures and discussions conducted with guest speakers from premier institutions.
